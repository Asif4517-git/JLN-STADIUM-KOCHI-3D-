About this simulation

A real-time, browser-native reconstruction of the Jawaharlal Nehru International Stadium, Kaloor, Kochi — home of Kerala Blasters and the 2017 FIFA U-17 World Cup final.

Modelled from the real ground & map data

  • Two-tier oval bowl with 24 seating bays and the scalloped PTFE membrane roof
  • Kaloor surroundings traced from OpenStreetMap: Stadium Road, Stadium Round, Stadium Link Road, the Old NH 47, Thevara–Perandoor canal, 798 real buildings, 1 317 road segments, the Kochi Metro viaduct and the three real metro stations
  • FIFA-standard 105 × 68 m pitch, goals, LED boards, dugouts and the west-end big screen
  • Manjappada in the yellow wall — Malayalam banners (മഞ്ഞപ്പട · എന്നും യെല്ലോ), surfer flags and 12 000 supporters

The match

  • Kerala Blasters in the real yellow home kit by Reyaur — Medhaa on the chest, Batery on the back, Polycab on the right sleeve, Salpido on the left, Manjappada on the collar
  • Full 11-a-side simulation against Mohun Bagan Super Giant, with passing, dribbling, tackles, saves, throw-ins and goals
  • ISL branding on the sleeves, the LED perimeter and the big screen — final score: Kerala Blasters win

Controls

  • W A S D fly · Space / C climb & descend · Shift boost
  • Drag to look around (mouse or touch) · Scroll zoom (FOV)
  • 1 / 2 / 3 drone · orbit · cinematic tour · R reset · H hide UI
  • N toggles night lighting · viewpoint chips for instant shots

Built with

three.js r149 (bundled, fully offline) · procedural canvas textures · embedded Noto Sans Malayalam & Oswald · real OSM geometry · no external assets, no network calls.
